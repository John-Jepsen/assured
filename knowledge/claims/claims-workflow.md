# Filing and Tracking a Claim

When a covered loss happens, the first step is to file a **first notice of loss
(FNOL)**. You provide the policy number, the type of loss, the date it occurred,
and a description. Filing a claim starts a review; it does not by itself guarantee
that the loss is covered or that a payment will be made.

After a claim is filed, an **adjuster** is typically assigned within one business
day. The adjuster reviews the details, may request documents such as photos of the
damage, a repair estimate, or a police or incident report, and determines whether
and how the loss is covered under the policy.

Typical claim statuses are: filed (FNOL), under review, information requested,
approved, denied, paid, and closed. You can check your claim status at any time
using your claim number. If information is requested, providing it promptly helps
the claim move forward.

If you disagree with a claim decision, you can dispute it. A dispute opens a review
by a licensed claims representative. The assistant can open a dispute and escalate
it, but it cannot overturn or guarantee a claim outcome.
