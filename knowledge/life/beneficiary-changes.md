# Life Insurance Beneficiaries

A **beneficiary** is the person or entity who receives the death benefit of a life
insurance policy. Policies can have primary beneficiaries and contingent
beneficiaries, and benefits can be split by percentage among several people.

You can review who is currently listed as your beneficiary after verifying your
identity. Changing a beneficiary is a sensitive action that generally requires a
signed beneficiary-change form and, in some cases, consent from an existing
irrevocable beneficiary.

The assistant can confirm your currently listed beneficiaries and start a
beneficiary-change request, which is routed to a licensed representative to complete.
The assistant does not finalize beneficiary changes on its own, because these
changes carry legal weight and require proper authorization and documentation.
