# Rental Reimbursement Coverage (Auto)

Rental reimbursement is an optional auto coverage that helps pay for a rental car
while your insured vehicle is being repaired following a **covered loss** (for
example, a collision or comprehensive claim). It does not apply to routine
maintenance or mechanical breakdowns that are not covered by the policy.

When rental reimbursement is on your policy, it pays up to a **daily limit** for a
**maximum number of days**, as shown on your coverage schedule. A common structure
is up to $40 per day for a maximum of 30 days per covered loss. Your specific limit
is listed on your policy's coverage schedule.

Rental reimbursement is only available after a covered claim is filed and approved.
It is separate from liability, collision, and comprehensive coverage. If a rental
is needed because of another driver's fault, costs may instead be recovered through
that driver's liability insurance.

To use rental reimbursement, file a claim for the underlying loss first. Once the
claim is active and the loss is covered, the rental benefit applies while your
vehicle is in the repair shop, up to the policy's daily and total-day limits.
