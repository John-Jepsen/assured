# Common Insurance Terminology

**Deductible** — the amount you pay out of pocket on a covered claim before your
insurance pays the rest. A $500 collision deductible means you pay the first $500.

**Premium** — the amount you pay for your policy, billed monthly, quarterly, or
annually.

**Limit** — the maximum amount a policy will pay for a covered loss. Limits can
apply per person, per incident, or per policy period.

**Exclusion** — something a policy specifically does not cover. For example, many
homeowners policies exclude flood and earthquake damage.

**Coverage** — a specific protection within a policy, such as liability, collision,
comprehensive, or dwelling coverage.

**Beneficiary** — the person or entity designated to receive a life insurance
benefit. Beneficiary changes are handled through a licensed representative.

**First Notice of Loss (FNOL)** — the initial report that starts a claim.

**Coinsurance** — on health plans, the percentage of costs you share after meeting
your deductible (for example, 20%).
