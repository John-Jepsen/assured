# How to Escalate to a Human Representative

Escalation is the process of handing a conversation from the assistant to a licensed human representative. It exists because some requests need professional judgment, involve a dispute, or fall outside what an automated assistant should decide. You can ask to speak with a person at any point, and the assistant will begin the handoff without argument. Asking for a human is always a valid request, and you do not need to justify it.

Some situations trigger escalation automatically, even if you do not ask. A disputed or contested claim is a common example, because resolving a disagreement about a claim outcome requires a person who can review the file and exercise discretion. Complaints, requests that touch underwriting or eligibility, and questions the knowledge base cannot answer safely are also routed to a human by design. In these cases the assistant explains why it is escalating so that the handoff does not feel abrupt.

When an escalation happens, the assistant summarizes the conversation and the reason for the handoff so the representative has context. This means you should not have to repeat everything from the beginning, although the representative may confirm a few details for security. Any identity verification you completed helps the representative pick up where the assistant left off. The goal is a smooth transition rather than a restart.

Escalation does not mean the assistant failed. It is a normal and expected part of good service, and it reflects a boundary that keeps sensitive decisions with qualified people. For straightforward tasks such as checking a balance, reviewing coverage, or making a test payment, the assistant can usually finish the job on its own. For anything involving judgment, exceptions, or disagreement, escalation is the safer and faster path to a resolution.

If you are ever unsure whether your issue needs a human, you can simply describe what you are trying to do and let the assistant suggest the best route. It will either handle the request directly or explain that a representative is better suited and offer to connect you. Either way, you remain in control of the conversation and can request a human whenever you prefer.
