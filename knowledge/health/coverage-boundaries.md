# Health Insurance: What the Assistant Can and Cannot Do

The assistant can explain general plan concepts and look up plan details on file,
such as your plan type (for example, a PPO), your deductible, and your coinsurance
share. It can tell you what your coverage documents say about in-network versus
out-of-network care.

The assistant cannot provide medical advice, determine medical necessity, tell you
whether a specific procedure will be approved, or quote exact out-of-pocket costs
for a future treatment. Those depend on clinical review, provider billing, and your
claims as they process.

For questions about whether a specific treatment is covered, pre-authorization, or a
denied medical claim, the best path is a licensed representative or your plan's
member services. The assistant can look up your plan's general terms and escalate to
a representative, but it will not guess about medical coverage decisions.
